Method and gaps

the run is for the most recent complete UTC day, 21st Oct 2026, generated on 22nd Oct.

Ranking source: the Wikimedia pageviews top endpoint for English Wikipedia, all-access, 21st Oct 2026 (full list, not the featured feed). Excluded non-article pages, with their true ranks and views: rank 1 Main_Page (6,469,109), rank 2 Special:Search (963,739), rank 3 Wikipedia:Featured_pictures (570,358). Ranks 4 to 23 are all articles, so the rank numbers in the table are the true ones.

Baselines: daily per-article views (all-access, user agent) for 24th Sep to 21st Oct, 28 days. The baseline is the median of days below twice the 27-day median before 21st Oct (a quiet-day median), so that earlier surges do not inflate it. Main Page, Deaths in 2026 and PHP2Shell use no surge adjustment (PHP2Shell has one day of data). Multiples and peaks were recomputed with a script from the same data.

Edits, protection and talk pages: the MediaWiki API for each article and its talk page, 24th Sep to 21st Oct, up to 500 revisions, with protection log entries. Reverts are revisions carrying a revert, undo or rollback tag. The Deaths in 2026 query hit the 500 cap.

Main Page banner: the featured feed for 21st Oct supplied the featured article and picture of the day.

Not verified:

  • Rank history on earlier days; only the 21st Oct list was pulled.
  • Most news items rest on one source or a search snippet; Amy Acton's CNN item, Clayface first reactions, the Elizabeth Holmes September spike and the Trump endorsement were not opened in full.
  • Automated traffic, platform mix (mobile or desktop) and referral sources were not checked, which matters most for ChatGPT.
  • Main Page exposure: Hongwu Emperor and the hoverfly picture are the only featured items checked.
  • The Heath Ledger tribute broadcast and the Como v Manchester United result were not confirmed.
  • The pageviews.wmcloud.org tool was unreachable and was not retried.
  • Kim Jong Un: no mainstream confirmation of the rumours was sought beyond one search.
