Method and gaps

Ranking source: the full top-1000 English Wikipedia pageviews list (all-access) for 5th Nov 2026, from the Wikimedia REST API. Non-article pages were excluded and the first 42 articles were taken; the 42nd article ranks 45th.

Excluded non-article pages (rank, views): 1 Main Page (4,172,050); 3 Special:Search (764,882); 6 Wikipedia:Featured pictures (561,738); 54 Portal:Current events (66,153); 113 File:Traffic Sign GR - KOK 2009 - R-54.svg (41,469); 209 Wikipedia:Village pump (technical) (25,479); 244 File:Traffic Sign GR - KOK 2009 - Π-26α.svg (22,612); 274 File:WhatsApp.svg (21,293); 311 Help:IPA/English (19,145); 508 Special:RecentChanges (13,616); 529 Special:Watchlist (13,186); 530 Wikipedia:Contact us (13,177); 610 Wikipedia:Signs of AI writing (12,033); 612 Wikipedia:About (12,027).

Baseline method: for each article, the median of the 14 lowest daily counts in the 28-day window (9th Oct to 5th Nov 2026, user traffic, all-access). This is a whole-window method, not a pre-surge median, so for long-running storylines the baseline is the quieter half of the window. Multiples are 5th Nov divided by that baseline. The Main Page comparison uses the plain 28-day median.

Continuing storylines: 32 of the 42 articles are in the storyline memory and are marked continuing. Annie Andrews was stored under the title Annie Andrews (pediatrician); that article was moved to the title Annie Andrews at 02:09 UTC on 4th Nov, so the storyline is treated as the same one and the alias should be added.

News research: done by seven parallel helper agents (six articles each) with WebSearch and WebFetch, following the minimums of at least 5 searches, 3 fully opened pages and 2 independent sources per stated cause. The Wikipedia data (views, edits, protection, Talk pages) was collected by the main run in the built-in browser.

Not verified:

  • Spot check: 9 cited URLs were re-opened by a separate agent after writing (AJC, OpenAI status, Fox News, Outlook India, TechRadar, CBS Atlanta, Texas Tribune, NBC Washington, CBS Austin); all 9 loaded and matched the cited headline and date.
  • Talk page threads were taken from edit summaries and then checked against the current page and Archive 1 to 6 only; threads missing from all of them are shown as removed or as not on the page, and the editor who removed or archived them was not identified except where stated.
