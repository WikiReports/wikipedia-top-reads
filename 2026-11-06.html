Method and gaps

Views come from the Wikimedia daily pageviews series for English Wikipedia, covering 10th Oct to 6th Nov (28 days) for the Main Page and each of the 42 articles. The ranking uses the full list of the top 1,000 pages for 6th Nov (UTC) and leaves out pages that are not articles; the 14 excluded pages are listed in the last section. Each baseline is the median of 10th to 23rd Oct, before the surges, except for Elizabeth Holmes and Jailer 2, which use the quiet days of 10th to 14th Oct because they were already high in the first half of the window. Multiples are the 6th Nov count divided by that baseline. Edits, page protection and talk-page threads come from the MediaWiki API for each article over the same 28 days. News was researched by seven helper researchers with web search, one per group of six articles; the main session checked the Main Page feed itself and re-opened nine of the cited news pages (Sacnilk, WLBT, TMZ, Sky Sports, PBS, ABC7 New York, the Metropolitan Police, The Week and Dark Horizons), and all nine supported the claims they were cited for, two of them as estimates rather than final figures. Storylines for continuing articles come from the stored memory of earlier reports and are shown with the 6th Nov entry added last.
