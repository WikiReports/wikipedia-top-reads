Method and gaps

This test run covered the top 20 articles by true rank on 20th Oct (UTC), ranks 4 to 23 of the full list, with a 28-day window of 23rd Sep to 20th Oct. Entries 1 to 10 were built from the official feed and checked against the full list; entries 11 to 20 were added in a second pass.

  • Ranks and views: ranks come from the Wikimedia top-1000 pageviews list for 20th Oct, with Main Page, Special:Search and Wikipedia:Featured pictures removed. Daily views come from the Wikimedia per-article pageviews data, all platforms, human users only.
  • Rank history: for entries 1 to 10 this comes from the feed, which lists only about the top 44 articles, so a day with no rank means the article was below that line, not that its views were zero. Entries 11 to 20 show only the 20th Oct rank.
  • Edits, protection, talk pages: read from Wikipedia's revision and protection logs for 6th-20th Oct. Deaths in 2026 hit the 500-edit query cap, so its edit count is a floor.
  • News: taken from search-result text. I did not open the full articles, so quotes and figures are as the outlets reported them.

Not verified

  • Why the official feed skipped ChatGPT (rank 10). Ranks 1 to 3 are the three non-article pages listed above.
  • Automated traffic: no user-versus-all-agents or platform-mix comparison was run for any article.
  • Main Page exposure (featured article, in the news, on this day) was not checked.
  • The Pageviews tool site could not be opened, so rank history relies on the feed alone.
  • 21st Oct data is not published yet, so Kim Jong Un's views may not have peaked.
  • No trigger was found for ChatGPT, Abdul El-Sayed's jump on 18th Oct, or Limonene's step up on 13th Oct.
  • Baselines for entries 11 to 20 are medians of all 28 days, not of quiet days only.
