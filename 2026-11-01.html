Multiples are views on 1st Nov divided by that median. Edit and talk-page counts cover 5th Oct to 1st Nov (the API query caps at 500 revisions, which affects Deaths in 2026, You Can See Everything and the Israeli election).
  • Storyline memory: 28 articles were matched to data/storylines.json in the website repository by canonical title and marked as continuing; 14 are new. The Storyline Tracker doc was not read for this run (the repo data was complete and used alone) and was not updated in this run.
  • News research: seven helper agents researched six articles each with web search and page fetches. The shared web-search budget ran out part-way, so the stated minimums (at least 5 searches, 3 opened pages and 2 independent sources per article) were not met for many articles; see the Not verified list. No claim rests on a search snippet alone unless marked.
  • Featured article and picture of the day came from the Wikipedia featured feed; the featured article's own rank and views were not checked.
