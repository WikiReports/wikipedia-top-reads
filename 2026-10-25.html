Method and gaps

Ranking source. The full top-1000 list from the Wikimedia pageviews API for 25th Oct 2026 (all-access, UTC), not the featured feed. Per-article daily views use the user agent type for 28th Sept to 25th Oct. Edit histories, talk pages and protection logs come from the English Wikipedia API. pageviews.wmcloud.org was not retried because it has been unreachable in earlier runs. The pageviews API returned rate-limit errors twice (Deaths in 2026 and Dorothy (film)); both were fetched again after a pause.

Research depth. Each of the 20 articles was researched by one of five parallel helpers, with at least five searches and three opened pages per article and two independent sources where they existed. Six helper claims were spot-checked by opening the cited pages, and all matched.

Excluded non-article pages above or near the cut-off. Main Page (rank 1, 7,038,796 views), Special:Search (2, 912,885) and Wikipedia:Featured pictures (3, 595,088). Twelve more lower-ranked non-article pages are listed in the final section.

Baselines. The median of the 27 days before 25th Oct for each article (a whole-window median, because most pages have no clean quiet stretch). Multiples are 25th Oct views divided by that median. Billy Evans (businessman) has only six days of data and so no baseline. Dorothy (film) is also described against its quiet days of 15th to 22nd Oct.
