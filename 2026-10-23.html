  • Excluded non-article pages (rank, views): Main_Page 1 (6,305,013); Special:Search 2 (966,449); Wikipedia:Featured_pictures 3 (555,054); Portal:Current_events 38 (58,705); File:Traffic_Sign_GR_-KOK_2009-R-54.svg 107 (30,173); File:WhatsApp.svg 202 (21,917); Help:IPA/English 282 (16,762); File:Traffic_Sign_GR-KOK_2009-_Π-26α.svg 293 (16,507); Wikipedia:Contact_us 304 (16,091); Wikipedia:About 334 (15,131); File:WNBA_logo.svg 432 (12,787); Special:RecentChanges 533 (11,013); Special:Watchlist 621 (10,044); Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing 740 (8,997). All 14 are listed in the last section.
  • Daily views: per-article endpoint, all-access, user agent, 26th Sep to 23rd Oct 2026 (28 days). Baselines are medians of the quiet days before a surge, with the day range stated for each article; where the page was never quiet the 28-day median was used (Holmes, ChatGPT, Deaths in 2026, Ansel Adams, Miranda Hart, XXX: Return of Xander Cage). PHP2Shell has only three days of data and no baseline. Multiples were recomputed with a script from the stored series.
  • Edits and protection: MediaWiki API revisions, protection info and protection log for each article and its talk page, 26th Sep to 23rd Oct. Revert counts are edits carrying revert, undo or rollback tags. Accounts shown as ~2026-… are temporary accounts, so anonymous edits are not separable by IP.
  • Main Page median: the 28-day median of the Main_Page series (26th Sep to 23rd Oct) is 6,458,295.
  • The pageviews.wmcloud.org tool was not used because it has been unreachable.

Not verified

  • Most news evidence comes from search snippets and headlines; only the pages linked with specific facts above were opened. CNN, Hollywood Reporter and Box Office Pro pages could not be fetched.
  • Tom Skerritt: one hoax-tracker page conflicts with the Fox News report of a family-confirmed death on 21st Oct; not resolved beyond preferring Fox and Wikipedia.
  • Triggers not found: Ansel Adams, XXX: Return of Xander Cage, United States, Death of Kyle Plush, the rise since 19th Oct for ChatGPT, and the 22nd Oct rise for Ilhan Omar.
  • Wildwood release date and Jailer 2 release date were inferred, not confirmed from a primary source.
  • Automated-traffic and platform mix (mobile versus desktop, bots classified as users) were not checked, and neither was Main Page link exposure, for example the In the news or Did you know boxes.
  • Rank history for earlier days was not pulled.
