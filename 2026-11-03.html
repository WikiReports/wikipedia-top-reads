Method and gaps

Ranks come from the full top-1000 list of English Wikipedia daily views for 3rd Nov 2026 (UTC, all-access, user traffic). Pages that are not articles are excluded, so an article's rank here is its true rank on the full list, which is why the table skips rank 30. Views for each article come from a 28-day daily series from 7th Oct to 3rd Nov. Baseline means the median of the quietest half (14 lowest days) of the 27 days before 3rd Nov, and a multiple is the day's views divided by that baseline. Edit, protection and talk-page facts come from the article histories and logs on English Wikipedia. Protection and talk-page status was checked on 4th Nov, so a thread shown as open, archived or removed reflects that later check. Talk-page threads reverted within 24 hours of posting are treated as noise and are not cited as evidence.

Causes are inferences from news coverage, edit activity and timing. Times after 00:00 UTC on 4th Nov belong to the next report, which matters for the election and the VisionQuest episode. Storylines continue from the project's stored notes; three stored notes are corrected here.
